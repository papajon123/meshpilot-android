# Testing MeshPilot through Google Play

[Русская версия](PLAY_TESTING.ru.md)

Google lets a new app go public only after at least **12 testers have kept it installed for 14 days in a row** in a
closed test. Joining takes two minutes and does not require a MeshCentral server. Feedback is welcome, but simply
keeping the app installed already helps.

## What you need

- An Android phone or tablet with Android 8 or newer.
- The Google account that is signed in to Google Play on that phone. Use the **same account** in every step below.

## Steps

1. **Join the test group:** open <https://groups.google.com/g/meshpilot-testers> and tap **Join group**. If the page
   shows a different account, switch to the one used by Google Play first.
2. **Become a tester:** open <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/io.github.papajon123.meshcentral> and tap
   **Become a tester**. The page must then say **You are a tester**.
3. **Install the app:** on the same page tap **download it on Google Play**, or open
   <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.github.papajon123.meshcentral> from the phone.
4. **Keep it installed for 14 days.** Do not leave the test program. Opening the app now and then is enough.

## If something does not work

- **Play search does not find MeshPilot.** That is expected: an app in a closed test is not listed in search. Use the
  links from steps 2 and 3.
- **"Item not found" or the app page does not open right after joining.** Access can take from a few minutes to a few
  hours. Try again later. Check that Google Play on the phone uses the account that joined the group (tap your profile
  picture in Google Play).
- **The link opens on a computer but not on the phone.** On the computer, open the app page from step 3 while signed
  in to the same account, tap **Install** and choose your phone. Play installs it remotely. Clearing the cache of the
  Google Play app (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage) also helps.
- **The APK from GitHub is already installed.** If Google Play refuses to install or update it, uninstall the GitHub
  version first, then install from Google Play.
- **Still stuck?** Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/papajon123/meshcentral-android/discussions) or open an
  [issue](https://github.com/papajon123/meshcentral-android/issues). Never post server addresses or credentials.
